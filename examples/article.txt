Many learners read English as a list of isolated words. They look up each word, write the Chinese meaning beside it, and move on. This feels productive, but it often leaves the sentence unchanged in their mind. A better habit is to ask what the word is doing in this sentence. Is it the main verb, a contrast, or a qualification? Once you can answer that question, the meaning becomes easier to remember. Context is not decoration; it is the instruction that tells you which sense to use.
